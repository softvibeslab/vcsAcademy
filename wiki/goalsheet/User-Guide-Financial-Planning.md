**Your Daily Sales Operating System** 🎯

## Welcome to the Future of Sales Performance Tracking

This digital system transforms your physical Goal Sheet into an interactive, gamified experience that helps you:

- 💰 **Hit Your Income Goals** with precise sales targets
- 📊 **Track Your Progress** day by day
- 💪 **Build Winning Habits** through daily attributes
- 🏆 **Earn Rewards** for consistent performance
- 📈 **Improve Continuously** with analytics and insights

## Quick Start (5 Minutes)

### Step 1: Set Your Financial Goal ⏱️ 3 min

1. Navigate to **Financial Planning** in the main menu
2. Enter your **Monthly Target Income** (e.g., $15,000)
3. Fill in your **Monthly Expenses** (17 categories):
   - 🏠 Rent/Mortgage
   - 🚗 Car Payment
   - ⚡ Electricity
   - 🛒 Food/Groceries
   - ... (13 more categories)
4. Click **"Calculate"** to see your targets
5. Click **"Save Financial Goal"** → Earn **+25 pts** 🎉

**What Happens Next**:
- System calculates your **Income Gap** (money you still need)
- Tells you exactly **how many sales** you need
- Tells you exactly **how many tours** you need
- Updates your dashboard with real-time progress

---

### Step 2: Understand Your Daily Routine ⏱️ 1 min

**Every day, you'll track 3 things**:

1. **📅 Sales** - Log each sale you make
2. **💪 Attributes** - Check off mindset habits you demonstrate
3. **🎯 Challenges** - Complete daily challenges (weekday-specific)

---

### Step 3: Start Your First Day ⏱️ 1 min

1. Go to **Daily Performance**
2. Click the **Attributes** tab
3. Check off attributes as you demonstrate them throughout the day
4. Log any sales in the **Sales Grid** tab
5. Try to complete all 7 attributes for **Daily Combo +100 bonus pts**!

## Common Questions

### Q: What if I don't make a sale today?

**A**: No worries! You can still earn points through:
- Personal attributes (up to 105 pts)
- Daily challenges (+25 pts)
- Total: Up to 130 pts without a single sale

---

### Q: Do I have to complete all 7 attributes every day?

**A**: No, but highly recommended! Here's why:
- **With Daily Combo**: +105 (attributes) +100 (bonus) = **205 pts**
- **Without Daily Combo**: Only ~60 pts (partial completion)
- **Difference**: 145 pts lost

**Strategy**: Aim for Daily Combo 5-6 days per week

---

### Q: Can I go back and log past days?

**A**: Yes! The system allows you to:
- Log sales for previous days in the month
- Complete attributes for today only
- Review past performance in Analytics

---

### Q: How do I increase my closing rate?

**A**: Use the Training Integration tab:
- Complete objection handling modules
- Practice closing techniques
- Apply learning on sales floor
- Track improvement in Analytics

---

### Q: What's the difference between Sales Grid and Analytics?

**A**:
- **Sales Grid**: Raw data entry (log each sale)
- **Analytics**: Processed insights (trends, predictions, recommendations)

**Use both**:
- Sales Grid → Daily logging
- Analytics → Weekly review

---

### Q: How accurate are the predictions?

**A**: Predictive Insights uses:
- Your historical performance
- Current progress
- Industry benchmarks
- Becomes more accurate over time

**Tip**: Log consistently for better predictions

## Troubleshooting

### Problem: "I can't save my financial goal"

**Solution**:
1. Ensure you filled in: Target Income + at least 1 expense
2. Check that Target Income > Total Expenses
3. Try refreshing the page
4. Contact support if persists

---

### Problem: "Daily Combo not awarding bonus"

**Solution**:
1. Ensure ALL 7 attributes show as achieved
2. Check that each attribute has notes (optional but recommended)
3. Refresh the page
4. Check your points total increased by 100

---

### Problem: "Sales not appearing in grid"

**Solution**:
1. Check you clicked "Save Record" after entering data
2. Verify the sale amount > 0
3. Check browser console for errors (F12 → Console)
4. Ensure backend service is running

---

### Problem: "Analytics shows no data"

**Solution**:
1. Must set financial goal first (Analytics depends on it)
2. Need at least 1 sale logged
3. Wait 24 hours for predictions to generate
4. Check Data Privacy settings if persists
